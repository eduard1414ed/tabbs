# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-27

The first version in the Chrome Web Store.

- The name is **Tabbs**. The toolbar icon follows the browser’s light or dark theme.
- Folders and spaces can have a pictogram: about 220 of them in 11 themes, or an emoji as before. Arc’s space icons come along with the import.
- **Spaces**, off by default: separate sets of links and tabs, switched with the icons at the bottom of the panel, a two-finger swipe or keys. Drag tabs, links and folders onto a space’s icon to move them there.
- The import from Arc in Helium goes through Helium’s own import, then Tabbs sorts what it brought.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-26

- English and Russian, depending on the browser’s language.
- ⇧⌘S (Ctrl+Shift+S) opens the panel.
- A welcome page with the setup steps for Helium.
- Import from Arc right in the panel, from Arc’s sidebar file.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-26

The first full version.

- Folders of links in the side panel, kept as ordinary bookmarks; a link opens its tab once and then switches to it.
- The other open tabs of the window below the line.
- Favorites as tiles on top.
- Rename, move, pin and unpin with a right-click or by dragging; undo after deleting.
- Folder emoji, sound on tabs, Open all / Close all, split view.
